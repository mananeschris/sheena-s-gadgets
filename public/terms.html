Clear terms for shopping, payment, and after-sales support.

These terms explain how website orders are submitted and reviewed. Product-specific price, stock, delivery fee, payment instructions, warranty, and plan details shown for your order also apply.

Product listings & orders

Please review the item, variant, quantity, listed price, and delivery details before submitting an order. Orders are subject to stock confirmation and shop review. If an item or order detail needs to change, the shop will contact you through the details on the order.

Payments & proof review

Available methods are shown at checkout and may include Cash on Delivery, GCash, installment, or layaway for eligible products. For GCash, upload the payment proof from your order page. A submitted proof is not confirmation of payment; the shop reviews it and updates the order. If proof is rejected, you may submit a corrected proof unless the order has been cancelled.

Delivery

Delivery areas, fees, and available arrangements are shown at checkout. Delivery timing depends on the destination and coordination with the assigned rider. Please provide a complete address, reachable contact number, and useful delivery notes.

Installment & layaway

An installment submission is a request, not an approval. The shop reviews the application and may request verification information. Layaway eligibility, downpayment, quantity limit, and payment deadline are shown for the selected product and plan. Stock is reserved only according to the plan's stated payment and confirmation process.

Cancellations & changes

Contact the shop as soon as possible if you need to correct or cancel an order. A request may be reviewed against the order and fulfilment status; an order is cancelled only when the shop confirms the cancellation. Any payment adjustment will be handled in accordance with the transaction details and applicable consumer rights.

Product concerns & warranty

Warranty coverage and claim steps depend on the product and the warranty terms provided with it. Submit a repair or service request from your customer account with the order details and a clear description of the issue. The shop will review the concern and explain the available next steps.

Consumer rights

Nothing in these terms removes rights or remedies available under applicable Philippine consumer-protection laws. Depending on the issue and the applicable law, remedies may include repair, replacement, refund, or another appropriate resolution. For help with an online-shopping concern, you may also visit the DTI Consumer CARe System.
