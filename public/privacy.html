Privacy Policy

What information the shop uses, and why.

This notice describes the customer information handled by the shop website for accounts, orders, payments, delivery, installment review, reviews, and customer support.

Account & contact details

The website stores details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, birthday, profile information, and saved addresses. These are used for account access, checkout, delivery coordination, and customer support.

Orders & payments

Order records include products, variants, quantities, delivery instructions, payment method, status, and related updates. GCash reference details and uploaded payment proofs are reviewed to verify payments and update the order.

Installment verification

If you apply for an installment plan, the form may collect your birthday, contact and address details, National ID number, ID photo, and selfie with your ID. These documents are used to review that application. Do not submit documents that the form does not request.

Reviews & uploaded media

Your rating, review, photos, and videos may be displayed on the product page so other customers can read them. Avoid including private contact, payment, or identity information in a public review.

Who may receive information

Authorized shop administrators and staff may access information needed to process orders, payments, service requests, and installment applications. Delivery name, phone number, address, and relevant instructions may be shared with the rider assigned to deliver your order.

Service providers

The website uses service providers to host and operate the system and to deliver account-verification and service emails. Information is shared with them only as needed for those services. GCash payments are reviewed from the details and proof you submit; this website does not collect your GCash MPIN or password.

Storage & retention

Order, payment, and support records are kept as needed to operate the shop, resolve concerns, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Identity documents should be kept only as long as needed for application review and related claims. This prototype does not publish a fixed retention schedule; ask the shop for the applicable period.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether your data is being processed, request access or correction, object to certain processing, or request erasure, blocking, or portability. You can update some account details from Account Settings. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission about data-subject rights.

Privacy contact: Sheena's Gadgets & Accessories Shop, serving Bacacay and Tabaco, Albay. For an account or order request, use the relevant customer profile or order page. A dedicated privacy contact and specific retention schedule should be added by the shop before this prototype is used for live customer data.
